# {{site_name}} Acceptable Use Policy

Version 2026-10 · 12 October 2026

This policy sets the rules for everyone who uses {{site_name}} ({{domain}}), the mailing lists run by {{organisation}}: the people who subscribe to its lists, and the people who write and send its messages. By subscribing, signing in or sending you agree to follow it.

## 1. Subscribers

1. Subscribe only with an email address that is yours. Do not subscribe another person, and do not confirm a subscription that is not yours.
2. Your sign-in links and the links in the messages you receive are personal. Do not pass on a link that lets someone act as you.
3. You can leave a list at any time: every message has an unsubscribe link, and an unsubscribe is acted on at once.
4. When you forward a message to someone:
   - forward only to people you know, who would expect to hear from you;
   - never use forwarding to send bulk or unsolicited mail.
5. When you use the contact form, a reaction link or your profile:
   - say who you are, and do not write as someone else;
   - do not send abuse, threats, harassment or anything illegal;
   - upload only a profile picture that you have the right to use and that is not offensive.
6. Do not try to get round the site's checks or limits, collect other subscribers' details, sign up addresses in bulk or by script, or interfere with the site.

## 2. Senders: permission to send

Senders are the administrators, and anyone else whose role lets them add subscribers or write and send messages.

1. Send only to people who asked to be on the list. That means:
   - they subscribed and confirmed it themselves, or gave their address in a way that made clear they would get this list's mail;
   - you can show when and how each person gave permission, if asked.
2. Adding or importing an address is not permission. A person gets a list's mail only once they have confirmed.
3. Do not add addresses that were bought, rented, swapped, scraped from websites or harvested in any other way.
4. Do not send to an old list that has not been mailed for more than 12 months without first asking the people on it to confirm.
5. A valid address is not permission. Address validation only checks that an address can receive mail.
6. Never put back a person who unsubscribed, bounced or complained, unless that person asks.

## 3. Senders: what you may not send

1. You may not use {{site_name}} to send:
   - spam or any unsolicited bulk or commercial email;
   - phishing, fake login pages, or mail that pretends to come from someone else;
   - malware, or links to malware;
   - scams, pyramid or get-rich-quick schemes, or fake invoices;
   - anything illegal in South Africa or in the recipient's country;
   - hate speech, threats, harassment, or content that promotes violence;
   - sexually explicit material;
   - anything that infringes someone else's copyright, trade mark or privacy.
2. Do not try to hide what you are sending, or to get round the checks, limits or suppressions of {{site_name}} or of the mail service that delivers its messages.

## 4. Senders: sending properly

1. Every message must say clearly who it is from, and its subject line must not mislead.
2. Every message to a list carries an unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe headers. {{site_name}} adds them: do not remove or hide them, and never mail a person again on a list they have left.
3. Send only from the sender addresses set up for {{site_name}}. Do not use someone else's address or domain.
4. Keep your sign-in private. You are responsible for everything sent from your account. If someone else may be able to use it, say so at once.

## 5. Limits and enforcement

1. Keep the lists clean. Mail is expected to stay below these rates:

| Measure | Warning | Action likely |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard bounces | 2% | 5% |
| Spam complaints | 0.1% | 0.3% |

2. Addresses that hard bounce, complain or ask for no mail at all are no longer mailed. Do not try to send to them again.
3. The mail service that delivers the lists has sending limits and rules of its own. Senders must keep to those as well.
4. If you break this policy, or what you do harms other subscribers or the reputation of the lists' mail, {{organisation}} may, with or without notice:
   - remove a message, a profile picture or a subscription;
   - slow down or stop a sender's sending, or take away a sender's role;
   - suspend or close your account.
5. Where possible you will be told why, and given a chance to fix the problem.

## 6. Data, law, changes and contact

1. Subscribers' details are used only to run the lists: to send their mail, record their choices and report on delivery. The Privacy page says more.
2. Senders are responsible for the personal information of the people on the lists, including obeying the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
3. Senders must also obey the direct-marketing rules of POPIA section 69, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act and the Consumer Protection Act, and the laws of every country they send to.
4. This policy is governed by the law of South Africa.
5. This policy may change. A new version gets a new version number.
6. Report abuse, and send questions about this policy, to {{contact}}.
