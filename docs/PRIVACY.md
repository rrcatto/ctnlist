# {{site_name}} Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10 · 26 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information {{organisation}} collects through {{site_name}} ({{domain}}), why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It follows South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 1. Who is responsible

{{organisation}} is responsible for the personal information processed through {{site_name}}. Send questions, requests and complaints about your personal information to our information officer at {{administrator_email}}.

## 2. What we collect

1. **Your email address**, when you subscribe, ask for a sign-in link or use the contact form, or when someone forwards one of our messages to you. It is the only thing we need: without it we cannot send you the lists' mail. Typed addresses are tidied up (for example, put in lower case), and addresses in some kinds of domain cannot be used for list mail.
2. **What you choose to add to your profile:** first and last name, telephone number, date of birth, gender, province, country, company, website and a profile picture. All of it is optional.
   - A picture is stored as a small square image made afresh from what you upload, so details hidden in a photo (such as where it was taken) are not kept.
   - Only you can see your picture.
3. **Your lists and your consent:** which lists you joined, when you confirmed, and when and why you unsubscribed.
4. **What happens to the messages we send you:**
   - when each one was sent;
   - whether it could be delivered, including bounces and spam complaints;
   - what you did with it on this site: opening it, liking or disliking it, forwarding it, confirming or unsubscribing through it, or contacting us from it. Messages may carry a tiny image that tells us when they are opened.
5. **An engagement score**, worked out from that activity and from when you last took part. It decides the order in which a message is sent to its list, and, when a message is limited to a number of people, who receives it.
6. **What you write in the contact form:** your name, email address and message, and, if you give them, your cell number, company, website and topic. Your IP address and browser are recorded with it.
7. **Addresses you forward a message to:** each person receives the message, and anyone not yet on our lists also gets an invitation to subscribe. Their address is added to our records, with a note that you forwarded the message to them. Forward only to people who would expect to hear from you.
8. **A record of each visit:** for every page (but not each automatic refresh of an administration page that is kept open), the IP address it came from, the host name of that address, the description your browser gives of itself, the page's address (without anything after a question mark), whether a form was sent, the site's answer, the time and, if you are signed in, your email address. When the sign-in or subscribe form is refused because it looks like the work of a program rather than a person, the email address typed into it is recorded with the visit, and no email is sent to it. When you sign in, the IP address and browser of the sign-in are recorded too, and the IP address of your latest sign-in is kept with your details.

We do not ask for special personal information (such as your health, religion or race), and the site is not meant for children under 18.

## 3. Why we use it

- To send you the lists you subscribed to, and only those.
- To sign you in with a link sent by email, and to keep you signed in.
- To record your consent and your choices, and to make sure we never mail someone who has unsubscribed or whose address cannot receive mail.
- To answer your messages.
- To keep the site secure and working: to limit repeated requests, look into abuse and put faults right.
- To see how our messages are received.

We send list mail only to people who subscribed and confirmed (POPIA, section 69), and every message has an unsubscribe link. We use the rest with your consent, because it is needed for the service you asked for, or in our legitimate interest in running a secure and lawful site.

## 4. Who else handles it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. These handle it for us, and only for us:

1. **Our email delivery service**, which sends every list message.
   - It receives your address and the message as it is written for you, which can include your name and links that are personal to you.
   - It reports whether the message was delivered, and it refuses to send to addresses that bounced, complained or asked it to send them nothing.
   - It may check whether an address can receive mail and, when we switch it on for a message, record opens and link clicks.
2. **Our mail server**, which sends sign-in links, invitations, confirmations of your choices and our answers to the contact form. Notices about your choices (confirming, unsubscribing with the reason you give, changes to your profile) are copied to the list's administrator.
3. **Our hosting provider**, whose servers hold the site and its database.
4. **The people who run the lists with us:** the administrators, and others only as far as their role allows. For example, someone who sends messages sees the addresses they go to.

Where {{organisation}} runs more than one list, its lists may share a record of addresses that unsubscribed from all lists or cannot receive mail, so that nobody who left one is mailed by another. They may also invite people who take part in another of its lists.

We give personal information to anyone else only when the law requires it.

**Outside South Africa.** Our servers and our email delivery service may be in another country. We use only providers that are bound to protect personal information as well as POPIA requires (section 72).

## 5. Cookies

The site sets two cookies, and both are needed for it to work:

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out, or until it expires |
| Session | Lets the site check that a form was sent from its own page, and carries short notes from one page to the next | Until you close your browser |

There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and the site loads no scripts or fonts from other sites. A message in the public archive may show pictures stored on another site, and loading them tells that site your IP address.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Your details, your lists and your consent history** are kept while the lists run, also after you unsubscribe: we need them to show that you agreed, and to make sure you are not mailed again unless you subscribe again.
- **The record of what we sent you** is kept with them.
- **Sign-in links** are deleted within a few hours of being used or expiring; **sign-ins** when they expire or you sign out.
- **Records of visits** are kept {{site_log_retention}}.
- **Contact form messages** are kept as our correspondence.
- **Full delivery reports** from our email delivery service are deleted after a few months; the outcome (delivered, bounced and so on) is kept.

## 7. Your rights and choices

1. **Unsubscribe** from any list with the link in each of its messages, or from all our lists at once: [manage your lists](/subscribe).
2. **See and correct your details:** sign in and open your profile. To change your email address, ask us.
3. **Ask what we hold:** what personal information we have about you, and who has received it (POPIA, section 23).
4. **Ask us to correct or delete it** (section 24). We delete your profile details and picture. We keep your address and consent history, marked as unsubscribed, only to prove your consent and to make sure you are not mailed again.
5. **Object** to our use of your personal information, or withdraw your consent, at any time (section 11). This does not undo what we did before.
6. **Complain** to the Information Regulator ([inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za)) if you think we have not handled your personal information properly. We would like the chance to put it right first: write to {{administrator_email}}.

Send requests to {{administrator_email}}. We may ask you to show that you are the person the information is about, and we answer within 30 days.

## 8. How we protect it

- The site is served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- There are no passwords to steal: you sign in with a single-use link that expires.
- The people who run the lists see only what their role allows.

## 9. Changes

When what we do with personal information changes, we update this policy. The version and date at the top show when it last changed.
